## 5. Business Setup Pages — Strategy (Next Lead Project)

### Why this matters
Many UAE prospects search for **company formation / free zone setup / trade licence** *before* they need an accountant. If RBO ranks for setup intent and then offers **setup + VAT + bookkeeping + corporate tax**, you capture the lead earlier and convert more successfully into long-term compliance clients.

### When we start
**Next project phase** — immediately after this SEO package (now live).  
This will be treated as a dedicated mini-project focused on lead generation.

### Proposed page structure
1. **UAE Business Setup** (hub page)  
2. **Mainland vs Free Zone** (comparison / decision page)  
3. **Dubai Free Zone / Mainland Setup**  
4. **Ajman Free Zone Setup** (strong fit with your Ajman Free Zone base)  
5. **Trade Licence Process** (step-by-step)  
6. **Business Setup Packages** — bundles that hand off into RBO services:
   - Setup + VAT registration  
   - Setup + Bookkeeping  
   - Setup + Corporate Tax registration  

### How these pages will generate better leads
- Target high-intent keywords (business setup UAE, free zone company formation, etc.)
- Strong CTAs: Free consultation / Free VAT Check / WhatsApp
- Internal links into existing VAT, Corporate Tax, Bookkeeping, Audit, and Payroll pages
- Clear positioning: *We don’t only form the company — we keep you FTA-compliant after setup*

### Note on services
If RBO partners for PRO/visa/bank introduction rather than delivering every step in-house, pages will be written honestly around **what you offer directly vs via partners**, to protect trust and compliance messaging.
